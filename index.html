// UNKNOWN FOUNDATION — PROTOTYPE INDEX

Three groups, one direction.

The selected site, plus the two exploration rounds it grew from. These aren't A/B tests — they're successive rounds: v1 explored the wordmark and mood, v2 built complete pages across four tonal themes, and the selected site synthesises the dark token field and the live terminal into one.

Every prototype opens in a new tab. The v1 directions and the selected site carry a live palette switcher — Amber · Graphite · Nocturne · Patina — at the bottom of each page. Judge the feel, not the pixel polish.

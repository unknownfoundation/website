Logo candidates - full exploration

Ship a two-mark SYSTEM rather than one logo, governed by the brief's Clarity/Credibility-first order. PRIMARY: Strata Horizon — it is the only mark that wins on all four axes at once (instant instrument read, fully ownable, zero misread, native 16px survival) and gives the Deep Amber a single honest target (the datum disc). It is the safest, most production-ready choice and the one I'd put in the header lockup beside the Geist Mono wordmark. SECONDARY / hero-and-favicon: pair it with The Benchmark Datum as the institutional seal (credibility, footer, favicon) and use Benchmark Summit as the atmospheric, editorial hero mark where size permits. These three share one DNA — a fixed datum point in characterized/unmapped ground — so they read as a coherent family across header (Strata Horizon), seal/favicon (Benchmark Datum), and hero (Benchmark Summit). Color rule across all: the surveyed ground stays warm-white on near-black; ONLY the fixed datum disc/dot takes Deep Amber. Next build steps: (1) render Strata Horizon, Benchmark Datum, and both required favicon cores at 16/24/32/48px on near-black AND warm-white to confirm single-color safety; (2) commit the favicon variants as separate files (do not auto-downscale the detailed marks); (3) build the header lockup with the wordmark and lock the mark-to-cap-height ratio; (4) run a blind 5-person misread test at 16px (target: nobody says 'letter,' 'gear,' 'crosshair,' or 'menu').

No mark in this set credibly reaches 9, and I would not claim one does. A 9 is a mark that is instantly legible, completely ownable, misread-proof at every size, AND carries the concept without a caption — the strongest here (Strata Horizon) is a genuine 8: it nails legibility, 16px survival, and register, but a faulted-section-with-dot is meaningful-once-explained rather than self-evidently 'Unknown Foundation,' which is the ceiling on its score. The cartographic marks (Benchmark Summit, Edge of the Map) are the most ownable and the closest to truly *meaning* the name, but they trade away the 16px robustness that a 9 requires, so they sit at 6.5-7.5. The realistic best outcome is an 8-level primary (Strata Horizon) shipped as a system with a credible seal (Benchmark Datum) and an atmospheric hero (Benchmark Summit). If you want to push for a 9, the highest-leverage move is a focused round that fuses Strata Horizon's bold 16px-safe geometry with Benchmark Summit's contour-narrative — a single bold contour shelf (not parallel strata) with the datum driven onto its high point — testing whether one mark can hold both the cleanliness and the unmistakable cartographic meaning. One caveat I'm flagging honestly: I verified geometry by tracing coordinates, not by pixel-rendering; before final ship, the refined SVGs must be rasterized at 16/24/32px to confirm the disc-to-shelf clearances I specified actually hold and that no stroke merges.
30 marks across the territories + research-direction marks. The 16px column is the favicon test. Switch palette at the bottom.

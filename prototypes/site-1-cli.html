// WHY WE EXIST

Manifesto

~/manifesto.md

We build experimental software in the open — alternatives to the tools we wish existed. We test ideas in public and publish what we find: the experiments, the dead ends, and the releases.

Open source is our research instrument. We favour long-term engineering over trend-chasing, and we survey technical territory that is not yet mapped, understood, or freely available.

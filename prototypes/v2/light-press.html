Expeditions

A public engineering journal.

Field notes from the work in progress. Each expedition documents a question we asked, what we built to answer it, what we learned, and where we go next. Written for engineers, by engineers.

  1. EXP-01
    Distributed Systems

    Distributed Intelligence: Can knowledge coordinate without a center?

    We asked whether a distributed system could maintain coherent, queryable knowledge across nodes without a central authority. What we found reshaped how we approached the Knowledge Engine's architecture.

  2. EXP-02
    Signal Architecture

    Routing at the edges: event flows without a message broker

    Standard message queues solve the wrong problem for small engineering teams. We prototyped a minimal routing layer that trades throughput guarantees for operational simplicity — then tested the tradeoffs under real load.

    2025-02 Published Read →
  3. EXP-03
    Engineering Memory

    Driftwood: treating institutional memory as infrastructure

    Engineering decisions decay silently. We explored what it would mean to version-control not just code, but the reasoning behind it — and began prototyping the system now running as Driftwood.
